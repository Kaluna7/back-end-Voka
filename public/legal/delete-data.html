Delete some of your Moocha data

You can delete specific data from your Moocha account without deleting the whole account. To delete everything, see Delete your account.

Delete it yourself in the app

Chat conversations

  1. Open the Chat tab.
  2. Select the conversation(s) you want to remove.
  3. Tap Delete and confirm. The messages are deleted from our servers.

Characters you created

  1. Open the character you created.
  2. Tap Delete and confirm. The character, its description and image are deleted.

Profile photo

  1. Open the Profile tab and tap your profile photo.
  2. Choose one of the built-in avatars (or another photo). Your previously uploaded photo is deleted from our servers.

Name

  1. Go to Profile → Account and change your name. The old name is overwritten.

Ask us to delete other data

For any other data, for example what an AI character or teacher remembers about you, your learning progress and scores, or your game history, email us from the address you use for Moocha:

To: kaluna12345678910@gmail.com
Subject: Delete some of my Moocha data
Tell us which data you want deleted and include the email address of your Moocha account.

We will verify the request, delete the data within 7 days and confirm by email. Your account stays active.

What is deleted and what may be kept

  • Data you delete is removed from our active systems right away, and from backups within 30 days.
  • Records we must keep by law (for example for fraud prevention or tax) are kept only as long as required.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google's policies.
