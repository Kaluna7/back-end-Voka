Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Moocha respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, and the choices you have.

Overview

Moocha is a language-learning app. We collect only the information needed to run your account, personalize lessons, and improve your experience.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Moocha, we may process:

  • Account details such as name, email, and profile photo.
  • Learning preferences such as target language and daily practice goal.
  • App activity including chat messages, game progress, and voice practice sessions.
  • Device and usage data needed to keep the app secure and reliable.

How we use your information

We use your data to:

  • Provide lessons, AI chat, games, and voice features.
  • Save your progress and sync settings across sessions.
  • Send optional reminders and account-related notifications you enable.

Photos and uploads

If you upload a profile photo, it is processed securely and stored for display in your account. We do not use your photo for advertising.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with service providers that help us operate Moocha, or when required by law. We keep data while your account is active and remove it when you delete your account, subject to legal obligations.

Your choices

You can update your profile, change notification settings, or delete your account from the Profile tab. If you sign out, your data remains stored until you delete the account.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Contact our support team through the app or your registered email address.
