Delete your Moocha account

You can permanently delete your Moocha account and the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Moocha and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: By email

If you can't open the app, email us from the address you use for Moocha:

To: kaluna12345678910@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my Moocha account
Include the email address of your Moocha account.

We will verify the request and delete your account within 7 days, then confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, profile photo and settings.
  • Your learning progress, skill levels, game results, points, coins and tokens.
  • Your chats with AI characters and teachers, and what the AI remembered about you.
  • Characters you created and your invitation records.

What may be kept

  • Data in backups is removed within 30 days.
  • Records we must keep by law (for example for fraud prevention or tax) are kept only as long as required.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google's policies. Deleting your account stops your subscription from renewing; you can also manage it in the Google Play Store.
